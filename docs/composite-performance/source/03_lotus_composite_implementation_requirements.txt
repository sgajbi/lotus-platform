# Lotus Composite Performance — Gold-Standard Product Requirements

**Document ID:** LOTUS-CMP-001  
**Version:** 2.0  
**Date:** 4 October 2026  
**Status:** Full product target for implementation and verification; not a statement of current completeness.  
**Audience:** Lotus implementation agents, product and methodology owners, engineers, QA, operations, compliance and auditors.  
**Scope:** Bank- and external-vendor-neutral; no inherited release restrictions or future-phase exclusions.

> Deliver a configurable, scalable composite-performance product whose membership, numbers, rules, source authority, approvals and publication history can be explained and reproduced. Meeting the supplied third-party requirements is the minimum; the complete target below includes richer analytics, migration, operating controls and configuration.

## How to use this specification

Every numbered requirement and capability catalogue in this document is part of the target. Implementation can proceed in dependency-ordered increments, but agents must not discard advanced capabilities as “outside Release 1,” silently replace them with placeholders, or claim the whole product is complete after delivering only calculation endpoints. A customer can disable a capability through configuration or entitlement without removing it from the product.

**SOURCE** means derived from the supplied text; **TARGET** means an added product requirement; **DECISION** means an institution must approve the specific policy before production use. All are requirements for implementation. A DECISION requires the supported options, validation and approval workflow to exist; it does not authorize an invented customer policy. Formulas and defaults below are proposed Lotus method contracts unless explicitly identified as a standards fact.

## 1. Product outcomes and invariants

### 1.1 Outcomes

| ID | Required product outcome |
|---|---|
| CMP-GEN-001 | Cover all neutral source requirements, including authoring, rule configuration, historical membership, composite returns, asset-class capabilities, analytics and distribution, regardless of source release restrictions. |
| CMP-GEN-002 | Support reusable institution/strategy/composite policies, configurable certified methods and governed extensions without institution-specific code forks. |
| CMP-GEN-003 | Support external-only, Lotus Core, and hybrid source profiles; migration can begin with raw accounting data, portfolio returns or approved historical composite series. Evidence depth must remain explicit. |
| CMP-GEN-004 | Support persisted, scheduled, event-triggered and on-demand calculations, with independent worker scaling and workload isolation. On-the-fly portfolio TWR alone does not satisfy composite operations. |
| CMP-GEN-005 | Support provisional, approved, frozen, externally attested, overridden, restated, superseded and withdrawn results with clear authority and reproducible history. |
| CMP-GEN-006 | Provide API-equivalent functionality for every user workflow, including authoring, simulation, imports, approvals, freezing, reports and inspection. |
| CMP-GEN-007 | Maintain separate business eligibility, source readiness, calculation state, approval/publication state and lineage depth. Normal business exclusions must not automatically mean a defective calculation. |
| CMP-GEN-008 | Prove claimed support with numerical, integration, security, operational and capacity evidence. A README, schema or endpoint name alone is insufficient. |

### 1.2 Invariants that configuration cannot disable

**CMP-INV-001.** Never silently drop an expected portfolio because its return, status, AUM or input file is missing. Distinguish a known policy exclusion from an unresolved data problem.

**CMP-INV-002.** Never substitute zero for missing history, interpolate an official investment return, link across a genuine track-record break, or manufacture portfolio constituents for an imported composite-only series.

**CMP-INV-003.** A later exclusion must not delete a portfolio's previously valid participation. Historical composite returns use the membership applicable to each period, not today's survivors.

**CMP-INV-004.** A published financial fact is immutable. “Overwrite” means an approved replacement version or authority selection, not destruction of the original. Unpublished drafts may be replaced through optimistic concurrency and an audit record.

**CMP-INV-005.** Freeze does not stop source ingestion or suppress known errors. It prevents unauthorized replacement of a selected official generation while corrections are investigated separately.

**CMP-INV-006.** Approval cannot make an invalid formula, incompatible history splice, incomplete eligible universe or prohibited compliance policy valid. Non-compliant analysis may exist only in explicitly non-official contexts with appropriate labels.

**CMP-INV-007.** No performance-based cherry-picking, retrospective optimization of eligibility to improve returns, fabricated upstream authority, cross-tenant access or hidden source fallback.

**CMP-INV-008.** Every reported figure must carry enough metadata to distinguish calculated, imported, overridden, simulated, stale and unavailable values. Unsupported metrics return typed reasons, not plausible-looking numbers.

## 2. Architecture and ownership

### 2.1 Reuse Lotus boundaries

The bounded repository inspection found composite-return calculation and materialization in Performance, and definition/membership products in Manage. This specification extends those boundaries; it is not authority to create a separate composite repository or a competing ledger. Reconfirm current contracts before implementation; the inspection was not a live certification. Repository evidence is in the companion review.

The table below is the **proposed target ownership map**. Only the bounded Performance and Manage evidence cited in the companion review was inspected for this task. Verify the other repositories and all cross-service contracts before assigning implementation work; this table is not evidence of their current functionality.

| Domain | Proposed accountable owner | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Canonical instruments, portfolios, valuations, transactions, source status history, prices and FX | `lotus-core`, with registered external producers where configured | Own source facts; do not infer composite performance or business eligibility. |
| Composite definitions, approved policy packs, eligibility evaluation, membership revisions and universe qualification | `lotus-manage` | Own membership authority; do not calculate investment returns. |
| Portfolio and composite returns, return ingestion/admission, weighting, linking, contribution, attribution and benchmark-return methods | `lotus-performance` | Own performance conclusions and certified financial methods. |
| Time-series risk, factor/exposure risk and scenario calculations | `lotus-risk` | Consume pinned inputs; do not reconstruct missing investment history. |
| Report assembly, templates, disclosures and report snapshots | `lotus-report` | Consume authoritative analytics; do not recalculate performance in templates. |
| Rendering | `lotus-render`, subject to its actual supported contracts | Render typed datasets; report assembly remains Report's responsibility. |
| Immutable evidence retention and retrieval | Domain stores and `lotus-archive` under explicit retention contracts | Archive is evidence custody, not another analytics authority. |
| Consumer-facing API orchestration | `lotus-gateway` | Propagate trusted identity and source/result references; no duplicate rule engine. |
| Configuration, review and report user journeys | `lotus-workbench` | Same permissions and APIs as non-UI consumers. |
| Deployment, identity integration, observability and shared operating controls | `lotus-platform` | Domain services retain responsibility for financial correctness. |
| Evidence-grounded narrative assistance | `lotus-ai` | Explain approved facts; never calculate authoritative returns or approve changes. |

**CMP-ARC-001.** Composite calculation workers may be separately deployed and scaled within `lotus-performance`. Avoid a new repository merely for scale isolation.

**CMP-ARC-002.** Separate deterministic domain engines from HTTP, persistence, queueing and UI concerns. Reuse certified return/risk methods instead of reimplementing them in reporting or a generic rule engine.

**CMP-ARC-003.** Expose stateless evaluation and governed managed execution. Stateless requests are not automatically official; publication requires source, universe, policy and approval evidence or an explicit externally attested publication route.

**CMP-ARC-004.** Domain integration uses versioned contracts and identifiers, not cross-service database joins. External-only operation must not require a fabricated Core portfolio or a live Core dependency.

**CMP-ARC-005.** Use one authority for each entity/fact and purpose. Replicated read models declare their origin and freshness; they cannot silently become source-of-record writers.

## 3. Domain model, time and states

### 3.1 Required logical entities

| Entity | Minimum contract |
|---|---|
| `FirmProfile` / `ReportingEntity` | Tenant, firm definition, business scope, compliance context, reporting currency and authorized approvers. |
| `CompositeDefinitionVersion` | Stable ID/code, purpose, strategy dimensions, creation/inception dates, effective interval, lifecycle and immutable digest. |
| `PolicyPackVersion` / `MethodVersion` | Typed resolved configuration, dependencies, standards applicability, author, approval and effective dates. |
| `SourceAuthorityProfile` | Fact-family, portfolio/universe scope, date range, authority, fallback/override policy and reconciliation contract. |
| `UniverseSnapshot` | Expected portfolio set, population owner, source cut/watermark, completeness and excluded/out-of-scope evidence. |
| `PortfolioAttributeInterval` | Typed value, business-effective interval, recorded time, source revision and lineage. |
| `RuleEvaluation` | Rule/version, target period, observations, threshold/operator, outcome, every reason and evidence references. |
| `MembershipRevision` | Immutable decisions for a complete declared universe, resolved policy, approval and coverage intervals. |
| `PortfolioReturnObservation` | Interval, value/unit, fee basis, currency, method, frequency, source authority and revision. |
| `MemberReturnFact` | Joined approved membership, return observation, weighting assets and both membership and accounting lineage. |
| `CompositePeriodResult` | Composite, period, fee/currency/method context, result, counts, assets, generation and supportability. |
| `CompositeSeriesSnapshot` | Ordered period-result version vector, track-record segment, continuity and coverage. |
| `MetricResult` | Metric/method version, value/unit/status, period, frequency, observations, dependencies and uncertainty. |
| `FreezeRecord` / `OverrideInstruction` | Scope, prior/new authority, rationale, evidence, approvers, expiry and concurrency token. |
| `CorrectionCase` / `RestatementCase` | Root cause, impact graph, old/new versions, materiality, approvals and redistribution plan. |
| `MigrationBatch` / `ReconciliationCase` | File/API manifest, schema/mapping version, source cut, counts, reconciliation and acceptance. |
| `ReportSnapshot` / `DeliveryReceipt` | Pinned financial versions, template/disclosures, digest, destination, attempt and acknowledgement state. |

**CMP-DOM-001.** All keys, caches, object paths, repository reads and durable references must enforce tenant and authorized firm scope. Composite overlap must not inflate firm assets.

**CMP-DOM-002.** Separate economic/effective time, valuation time, accounting/booking time, received time, calculation time, approval time and publication time. Preserve source timezones and timestamp precision.

**CMP-DOM-003.** Use an explicit internal interval convention, preferably `[valid_from, valid_to)`. Convert inclusive source periods at adapters. Retain the original convention; validate month boundaries, leap days, daylight-saving shifts and end-of-day versus beginning-of-day status changes.

**CMP-DOM-004.** Distinguish account opening, first funding, discretionary-management start, performance start, composite creation, composite performance inception, cessation and reactivation. Do not reset inception on rename or provider migration.

**CMP-DOM-005.** Support `AS_REPORTED(publication_id)`, `LATEST_APPROVED`, `EXACT_VERSION`, `AS_KNOWN_AT(timestamp)` and separately `LATEST_CALCULATED`. Reject unsupported temporal queries rather than infer missing recorded-time history.

### 3.2 Independent state axes

| Axis | Required semantic values; names may map to existing contracts |
|---|---|
| Assignment | Matched, out of scope, ambiguous, unknown |
| Rule | Pass, fail, unknown, not applicable |
| Business eligibility | Eligible, ineligible, undetermined |
| Source presence | Present, absent-but-expected, historical-only, confirmed terminated |
| Data readiness | Complete, incomplete, unavailable, invalid |
| Execution | Queued, running, waiting, failed, succeeded, cancelled |
| Publication | Draft, provisional, pending approval, official, superseded, withdrawn |
| Edit protection | Open, frozen, restatement-authorized |
| Financial authority | Calculated, external-attested, manually overridden, simulated |
| Evidence depth | Raw-input-reproducible, member-return-reproducible, composite-series-only |

**CMP-DOM-006.** Reconcile universe counts as disjoint included, policy-excluded, undecidable and out-of-scope populations. Source-presence flags and multi-reason counts are overlays: their totals need not equal portfolio count. An excluded portfolio may have several reasons and remains one portfolio.

## 4. Composite catalogue and lifecycle

| ID | Required behaviour |
|---|---|
| CMP-CAT-001 | Create, clone, validate, approve, schedule, activate, suspend, retire and inspect composites through APIs and UI. Pre-established catalogues are templates, not a platform limitation. |
| CMP-CAT-002 | Configure mandate, risk profile, model/model family, native currency, account type and typed attributes. Business unit, manager, fee band and jurisdiction can be reporting/analysis dimensions; official composite definitions require approved strategy/discretion relevance and cannot be based solely on an impermissible criterion. No proprietary strategy names in engine logic. |
| CMP-CAT-003 | Distinguish actual strategy composites, internal analytical cohorts, simulated/model tracks, carve-out composites and composites using MWR/alternative-asset methods. Purpose controls permissible methods and presentation. |
| CMP-CAT-004 | Support genuinely exclusive strategy families and overlapping applicable composites. Evaluate all relevant definitions and include portfolios wherever the approved standards/definition require; no first-match rule may omit required membership. Flag contradictory exclusive assignments. Deduplicate assets at firm level. |
| CMP-CAT-005 | Version rename, benchmark change, strategy redefinition, merger, split, manager change, currency change and cessation separately. Require a continuation/new-track decision; never concatenate incompatible histories automatically. |
| CMP-CAT-006 | Support minimum-asset rules, grace periods, closed/reopened composites and periods with zero members. A true no-member gap terminates continuity; display segments separately. |
| CMP-CAT-007 | Support composite hierarchies and presentation groupings. Roll up primitive portfolio facts with approved weights and deduplication; never average child-composite returns without a specified economic method. |
| CMP-CAT-008 | Retain all historical definitions, membership, descriptions, changes and terminated track records according to retention and legal-hold policies. Reports can access all available history, not a hard-coded five-year cap. |
| CMP-CAT-009 | Separate native strategy currency from a report display currency. A translated view is not automatically a new strategy composite. |
| CMP-CAT-010 | Permit backdated data corrections but prohibit a disguised retrospective definition change in official standards-bound history. A research reconstruction must be separately labelled. |

## 5. Configurable engines and policy management

### 5.1 Engine and policy registries

**CMP-CFG-001.** Provide versioned registries for assignment, eligibility, flow classification, temporal observation, re-entry, valuation, portfolio returns, composite weighting, period linking, annualization, fee normalization, FX conversion, benchmarks, contribution smoothing, risk, dispersion, attribution, quality, materiality, publication and report templates.

**CMP-CFG-002.** Each method version declares: identifier, owner, purpose, input/output schemas, units, accepted frequencies, numerical domain, dependencies, formula/specification, precision, determinism, complexity/resource limits, standards contexts, reference tests, compatibility and deprecation policy. Registry status is draft/validated/approved/retired; installation is not approval.

**CMP-CFG-003.** Resolve configuration from platform method catalogue → tenant/firm profile → strategy-family profile → composite version → permitted run parameters. Persist the complete resolved policy and digest. A permitted run parameter must not override a mandatory official policy or change a frozen series invisibly.

**CMP-CFG-004.** Business rules use a typed declarative language: boolean composition, comparisons, bounded set membership, interval overlap, count/min/max/average/sum, consecutive-day tests and approved derived measures. Disallow arbitrary executable code, SQL and unbounded expressions in business configuration.

**CMP-CFG-005.** New financial algorithms may be supplied through maintained, reviewed extensions with isolated execution, dependency/version controls and independent fixtures. Do not pretend an arbitrary formula typed into a UI is a certified financial method.

**CMP-CFG-006.** Validate units, currencies, null semantics, date coverage, input availability, conflicting conditions, impossible re-entry, dependency cycles, overlapping effective versions and duplicate authority. Reject configuration that cannot produce an auditable result.

### 5.2 Authoring and change control

**CMP-CFG-007.** Support visual editors, configuration-as-code import/export, schema validation, human-readable previews, inheritance inspection and semantic diffs. A schema migration must preserve old versions for replay.

**CMP-CFG-008.** Lifecycle: draft → validated → independent approval → scheduled/effective → superseded/retired. Approval binds exact content and attachments; any subsequent change invalidates approval. No material self-approval.

**CMP-CFG-009.** Provide an isolated simulation sandbox showing changed memberships, reasons, assets, periods, returns and data needs. Separate policy sensitivity from source corrections and market effects. Simulation cannot automatically select the best-performing policy for official history.

**CMP-CFG-010.** Support bulk deployment across composites with an impact preview, bounded batch size, explicit all-or-nothing versus per-item semantics, approval and rollback through a new version. A rollback never deletes intervening evidence.

**CMP-CFG-011.** Feature activation is configurable by tenant and composite, but audit, immutable publication, authorization, source completeness and standards guardrails are not optional toggles.

**CMP-CFG-012.** All customer-specific choices below must be expressible through policy, not custom code. The reference 10% cash-flow and 5% cash tests are profile values, not universal constants. The full product includes user configuration immediately as a required capability, irrespective of historical source release restrictions.

## 6. External, Core and hybrid data

### 6.1 Supported sourcing modes

| Mode | Required operation | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|
| External accounting inputs | Ingest external positions, valuations, transactions, FX and statuses; Lotus calculates portfolio and composite returns. | Mapping/admission establishes a real external authority; do not relabel as Core-origin data. |
| External portfolio returns | Ingest daily or monthly gross/net portfolio returns plus assets, eligibility and history. | Retain supplied return method, fee basis and provenance; no implied raw-input reproducibility. |
| External composite history | Import approved historical composite periods, disclosures and available constituent evidence. | Publish as externally attested history; missing constituents and unavailable metrics remain explicit. |
| Core sourced | Resolve pinned source products from `lotus-core`; compute or consume approved returns. | Contract, tenant, source cut, freshness and business-date evidence required. |
| Hybrid by fact family | For example, Core universe/status, external returns, external benchmark, Core FX. | One approved authority per fact key and purpose; compatible timestamps and bases. |
| Hybrid by period/cohort | External pre-cutover history, Core post-cutover; different books may transition separately. | Gap/overlap checks, reconciliation and approved cutover manifests. |
| Parallel validation | Two sources or engines calculate the same interval for comparison. | Shadow is not official; disagreement is a reconciliation case, not averaging. |

**CMP-SRC-001.** Declare authority at `(tenant, firm, fact_family, portfolio/composite scope, effective interval, purpose, fee view, currency)` with explicit precedence and version. Do not select whichever source happens to respond first.

**CMP-SRC-002.** Support full snapshots, incremental changes, event streams, API batches and files. Input manifests declare whether omission means unchanged, not-in-snapshot, deletion/tombstone or a broken delivery. Unknown file semantics block authoritative use.

**CMP-SRC-003.** Maintain an expected universe independently of the file's present rows. Reconcile expected, supplied, duplicate, missing, unexpected and terminated entities. A zero-row outage must not become an empty but complete composite.

**CMP-SRC-004.** External identifier mappings are effective-dated and reversible: portfolio, account, institution, mandate, instrument, benchmark and classification. Handle renumbering, mergers, reused identifiers and upstream corrections without duplicating history.

**CMP-SRC-005.** Source fallback is configured, pre-approved, visible and provenance-preserving. A fallback that changes financial authority or methodology requires the applicable approval; frozen results do not auto-switch to it.

**CMP-SRC-006.** Preserve original payload/file digest, source schema, ingestion/mapping version, source record/version, event ID, received time, declared business date, producer and normalized content digest. Validate signatures/checksums when supplied.

### 6.2 Canonical input contracts

| Fact family | Minimum required content |
|---|---|
| Portfolio identity/status | Stable ID, firm, source ID mapping, domicile/nationality or minimized eligibility flags, accreditation intervals, dates, discretionary status, fee agreement, closure/suspension, restriction/block flags and provenance. |
| Strategy binding | Mandate, risk profile, native currency, model family/version, effective dates and client-versus-manager change reason. |
| Valuation/AUM | Portfolio, observation time/date, currency, amount, basis, cash/receivable/accrued-income coverage, valuation status, source revision and quality. |
| Cash/cash equivalents | Amount, currency, approved classifications, inclusion of unsettled cash/receivables, encumbrance and calculation denominator. |
| Transactions/flows | Stable transaction and revision IDs, economic/booking/settlement dates, type/externality, signed amount/currency, asset quantity/value for in-kind flows, reversal linkage, fee/tax classification and source. |
| Return observations | Portfolio, exact period, decimal return and unit, currency, gross/net/model-fee basis, cumulative/annualized flag, frequency, method, source version and status. |
| Drift/model observations | Actual and target weights or approved drift measure, metric definition, model/taxonomy version, timestamps, cash treatment and coverage. |
| Reference series | Benchmark/risk-free IDs, levels or returns, total/price-return type, calendar, currency, hedging/tax basis, effective definitions and source rights. |
| Holdings/exposures | Instrument positions, values, classifications, issuer, sector, geography, currency, duration/rating/factors and derivatives exposure conventions. |
| Imported composite periods | Composite mapping, interval, return/fee/currency/method, publication status, official version, AUM/member counts/dispersion where supplied, provenance and evidence depth. |

**CMP-SRC-007.** Inputs use unambiguous units. Monetary and exact ratio inputs support decimal strings; convert percentage uploads explicitly. Retain original precision; never claim more accuracy than an external source supplied.

**CMP-SRC-008.** Validate uniqueness, completeness, chronology, interval overlap, base currency consistency, finite values, duplicate trade legs, cancellations and reversals. Backdated events generate impact notifications rather than quietly replacing official results.

**CMP-SRC-009.** Null, false, zero, empty, not-applicable and unknown have different semantics. Missing mental-incapacity, restriction or fee flags must not silently become “no restriction.” Minimize sensitive attributes in downstream stores and reports.

**CMP-SRC-010.** Income accruals, dividends, interest and receivable-to-cash movements must not be mistaken for external capital. An internal movement may be external to a carve-out but internal to the total portfolio; classify at the relevant measurement boundary.

**CMP-SRC-011.** External daily returns can be linked to monthly returns after coverage checks; externally supplied monthly returns cannot reconstruct daily volatility, daily drawdown or transaction-level attribution. Such analytics are unavailable unless independent appropriate inputs exist.

**CMP-SRC-012.** Readiness must be inspectable per source, portfolio, rule, period and metric. Missing market data for an optional metric does not invalidate a separately complete return, but missing a required eligible member return blocks a complete computed composite.

## 7. Migration and source cutover

**CMP-MIG-001.** Provide reusable migration mappings, dry runs, previews, validation reports, staged loading, resumable imports, quarantine/rejection handling and explicit promotion to governed authority. Support CSV/XLSX, structured JSON/API and bulk columnar formats through validated adapters, not handwritten one-off fixes.

**CMP-MIG-002.** Support full-history recomputation, portfolio-return history migration, composite-only history migration and mixed evidence depth. An imported official period may be frozen as the accepted legacy record while a recomputed shadow version is compared separately.

**CMP-MIG-003.** Migration manifests identify input files/products, checksums, source extraction time, record counts, relevant date ranges, portfolio mappings, currencies, fee bases, histories, policy versions, ownership and approvals.

**CMP-MIG-004.** Reconcile identity/population counts, beginning/ending assets, flows, portfolio returns, composite weights/returns, counts, horizons, annual dispersion and report values where those fields exist. Tolerances specify absolute/relative/basis-point units and comparison precision; no blanket “within tolerance” without field-level evidence.

**CMP-MIG-005.** Classify discrepancies: source-data, identity, date/calendar, FX, fee, rounding, method, eligibility, history completeness or unexplained. Unsupported historical evidence is not a matched result.

**CMP-MIG-006.** Handle pre-inception records, closed portfolios, archived status intervals, source renumbering, superseded transactions and historical restatements. Do not migrate only today's active book and call it historical composite reconstruction.

**CMP-MIG-007.** At cutover, pin the last legacy interval and first new interval, method/fee/currency compatibility, source authority transition, continuity segment, parallel-run differences and approvals. Overlaps require explicit winner selection; gaps remain visible.

**CMP-MIG-008.** Keep system migration separate from track-record portability between firms/managers. A successful data import does not authorize a portability or compliance claim; retain the evidence and decision applicable to the history's ownership.

**CMP-MIG-009.** Support rollback of an unpublished migration and superseding an accepted migration through a new version. Never delete previously distributed reports or orphan their referenced evidence.

**CMP-MIG-010.** Produce a migration acceptance pack and an export/exit pack. Export definitions, rules, mappings, membership, return observations, approved versions, freeze/override histories and lineage in documented formats so the institution is not locked into Lotus.

**CMP-MIG-011.** A member-return import must have its own genuine external authority/admission route. Do not forge a retained Lotus calculation ID to satisfy an existing internally sourced materialization contract.

## 8. Eligibility, assignment and re-entry

### 8.1 Evaluation contract

**CMP-ELG-001.** For each target period, pin the complete universe, effective definitions, policy, business calendar and source cut. Resolve strategy assignment; evaluate every applicable rule; retain all failures and unknowns; emit an immutable membership revision.

**CMP-ELG-002.** Each rule defines applicability, typed inputs and authority, input frequency, observation window, evaluation point, threshold/unit/operator, exclusion start/end, re-entry policy, missing-data outcome and reason codes. Daily observations may determine monthly membership without daily entry/exit.

**CMP-ELG-003.** Any applicable known failure makes the portfolio ineligible; otherwise a required unknown makes it undetermined; otherwise it is eligible. Unknowns remain in evidence even where another rule proves exclusion. No short-circuiting that loses reasons.

**CMP-ELG-004.** Under the source-reference profile, membership is finalized for the whole calendar month. Separate certified profiles may support other membership frequencies, but daily-derived monthly membership and an independently defined daily composite must not be confused.

**CMP-ELG-005.** Provisional results declare evaluated-through date, incomplete observation windows, provisional reasons and expected finalization. A temporary intramonth net-flow breach may disappear before month-end.

**CMP-ELG-006.** Re-entry requires all relevant failures to clear and every applicable next-period rule to pass. Resolving a block does not automatically clear drift, accreditation or funding failures.

### 8.2 Required rule catalogue

| Rule | Source-reference behaviour | Required configurable alternatives / unresolved decisions |
|---|---|---|
| Mandate/risk/currency mapping | Separate strategy tracks. Transition month excluded when these fields change. | Client-directed versus tactical/model changes; simultaneous changes; first/last-day changes; re-entry timing; same-strategy rename. |
| Accredited-investor status | Effective-dated evaluation; source leaves any-ineligible-day versus requalification unresolved. | Any-day, month-end, sustained-qualified-days and approved requalification+readiness policies. Preserve separate business-reporting/standards contexts. |
| Significant external flows | Net target-month flows at or above 10% of prior month-end AUM exclude that month. | Explicit signed versus absolute net; gross inflow/outflow; threshold, window and externality. Do not silently replace the source net rule with a transaction rule. |
| Core-strategy drift | Eligibility depends on drift; threshold 10% versus 15% and observation method unresolved. | Max deviation, half-L1, relative deviation; prior-end/any-day/average/consecutive-day measures; manager rebalancing treatment. |
| No-model direct-bond cash | Month-end cash ratio at most 5%. | Cash-equivalent definition, settled/unsettled treatment, denominator, liabilities and threshold comparator. |
| New account | Exclude performance-start calendar month; entry next full month after discretionary/funded/invested tests. | Full-period-first-day exception, mandate-specific grace, funding versus performance date, paused/reopened accounts. |
| Fee status | Zero management fee may exclude under the source business policy. | Include/exclude non-fee-paying portfolios by approved purpose; fee waiver, rebates, fee holidays and negative fee adjustments. |
| Closure/suspension | May exclude; preserve historical participation. | Last full managed period, timestamp/timezone, temporary suspension, reopened account and closure-file semantics. |
| Deceased/incapacity | May exclude under supplied policy. | Effective event versus received date, actual loss of discretion, restoration and sensitive-data minimization. |
| Transaction blocks | Investment/all-transaction blocks trigger exclusion. | Block type, asset-specific impact, partial-day impact, pending approval and clearance date. |
| Client/product/nationality restrictions | Material restrictions can exclude. | Materiality linked to strategy implementability; effective dates; rule applicability; nationality is not a universal standards exclusion. |
| Minimum assets/deployment | Funded and substantially invested; configurable minimum assets. | Measurement basis, prospective threshold change, below-threshold observation, funding grace and denominator quality. |
| Missing universe/input | Not specified in source. | Never infer closure; declare unknown/data failure and block affected official computation. |
| Temporary new account | Product enhancement. | Segregate significant-flow assets using an approved operational policy; retain boundaries and reconciliation, not fictitious cash accounts. |

**CMP-ELG-007.** Distinguish an investment manager's tactical drift/rebalance from loss of discretion caused by client restrictions. Outlier return or manager-controlled drift is a diagnostic, not automatic authority to discard bad performance.

**CMP-ELG-008.** Support overrides of individual rule decisions only through an allowed, time-bounded policy with rationale, evidence and independent approval. Preserve original machine evaluations. An override cannot supply missing data or bypass standards restrictions.

### 8.3 Drift and cash semantics

**CMP-ELG-009.** A drift definition must specify dimension/taxonomy, model version, actual/target weight bases, cash, look-through, leverage/shorts, observation and zero targets. For target weights `t_j` and actual `a_j`:

```text
MAX_ABSOLUTE_DEVIATION = max_j abs(a_j - t_j)
HALF_L1_DEVIATION      = 0.5 * sum_j abs(a_j - t_j)
```

Relative drift requires an explicit rule for `t_j = 0`. Do not call percentage-point drift a percentage relative difference. A metric, threshold and comparator are inseparable configuration.

**CMP-ELG-010.** Cash eligibility defines the numerator (cash, cash equivalents, accrued income, unsettled trades and encumbrance treatment) and denominator (net assets, investable assets or another certified basis). Missing or nonpositive denominators are typed failures, not 0% cash.

### 8.4 Significant-flow contract

**CMP-FLW-001.** Net all qualifying external transactions over the complete target month using a versioned business-type mapping. Source terms such as Transfer, Incoming Payment, Money Transfer and Payment require semantic mapping; internal transfers are not automatically external.

**CMP-FLW-002.** Support signed-net, absolute-net, gross-inflow, gross-outflow and gross-total measures as distinct policies. The recommended resolution for symmetric deposits/withdrawals is absolute net, but the source does not explicitly settle the sign convention.

```text
net_flow_M = sum(signed_qualifying_flows_converted_to_reference_currency)
ratio_M    = abs(net_flow_M) / prior_month_end_AUM    # absolute-net profile only
exclude_M  = ratio_M >= threshold
```

**CMP-FLW-003.** Pin flow date basis, timezone, holiday treatment, FX date/source, in-kind valuation, cancellation/reversal and duplicate treatment. Net only after currency normalization. Retain gross diagnostics even when the policy uses net flows.

**CMP-FLW-004.** A zero, negative or missing denominator requires an explicit invalid/first-funding policy. Do not use today's AUM as an undocumented substitute.

**CMP-FLW-005.** An individual flow exceeding 10% is not a final exclusion where subsequent monthly flows net below the threshold. Re-entry for month M+1 uses the appropriate M-end readiness plus M+1 rules; exclusion is not automatically carried forward.

**CMP-FLW-006.** Keep significant-flow eligibility policy separate from large-flow valuation requirements within portfolio TWR. Both must have independently defined, compatible method/policy configurations.

## 9. Return engines and financial methods

The formulas in this section specify proposed Lotus engine contracts. They are not a claim that GIPS prescribes one formula for every situation. Each method must be certified for its intended use, with applicability mapped to the standards register in section 12. All example returns use decimal ratios: `0.02` means 2%.

### 9.1 Portfolio-return admission and calculation

**CMP-RET-001.** Support both supplied portfolio TWR and Lotus-calculated portfolio TWR. Preserve whether a return is independently calculated, imported, externally attested or manually corrected. An upstream return must carry method, currency, fee basis, dates, precision and coverage evidence. Publication additionally requires the applicable completeness and approval gates.

**CMP-RET-002.** For genuinely complete daily observations within month M, link portfolio returns as `r_i,M = product_d(1 + r_i,d) - 1`. Do not average daily returns, substitute a monthly return for a missing day, double-count overlapping intervals, or create weekend returns unless the approved calendar explicitly defines them.

**CMP-RET-003.** Reuse the portfolio TWR engine with documented external-flow timing, valuation boundaries, accrued income, fees and large-flow treatment. Support exact subperiod valuation when available and approved approximation methods when their prerequisites hold. Modified Dietz is an approximation, not automatically exact TWR.

For a supported Modified Dietz subperiod:

```text
r = (V_end - V_begin - sum_j CF_j) /
    (V_begin + sum_j alpha_j * CF_j)
```

`CF_j` is positive into the measured portfolio; `alpha_j` is the fraction of the subperiod for which that flow is invested under the approved beginning/end-of-day convention. Derive coefficients from real dated flows, not transaction row order. Invalid denominator or insufficient large-flow valuations must not be concealed by an arbitrary fallback.

**CMP-RET-004.** The portfolio engine must distinguish market loss, capital withdrawal, fee debit, income accrual, corporate action and internal transfer. A dividend receivable followed by cash settlement is not two income events and is not an external subscription. Portfolio cash, accrued income and residual assets remain part of performance under the adopted accounting policy.

**CMP-RET-015.** Raw-input calculation profiles must validate the approved fair-valuation hierarchy, trade-date accounting, accrual treatment, income and cash inclusion, business-calendar valuation frequency and large-flow valuations. Liquid and private/illiquid strategies require their applicable approved valuation calendars; the latter must not be forced through an unjustified daily observed-price assumption. Missing or stale prices and estimated valuations must be explicit. These controls apply to external return admission through attested method/evidence checks as well as internally calculated returns.

### 9.2 Composite weighting methods

Let `E_M` be the final eligible portfolio set for month M, `A_i,0` its beginning assets, and `r_i,M` its return. All weights and returns must have compatible dates, currency and fee views.

| ID | Engine | Required calculation and controls |
|---|---|---|
| CMP-WGT-001 | Beginning-asset-weighted | `w_i,M = A_i,0 / sum(E_M assets)`; `R_M = sum_i(w_i,M * r_i,M)`. Preserve exact numerators, denominator, weights and contributions. Never use ending assets silently. |
| CMP-WGT-002 | Beginning assets plus weighted external flows | Replace the weight numerator with `B_i = A_i,0 + sum_j(alpha_i,j * CF_i,j)`, then normalize across eligible portfolios. Require the approved method, complete dated flows, timing coefficients and valid denominators. |
| CMP-WGT-003 | Aggregate composite method | Construct a composite measurement unit from complete compatible portfolio valuations and external-flow data, then apply the certified portfolio method to the aggregate. Eliminate flows internal to that measurement boundary without losing audit evidence. Do not infer this result from AUM change alone. |
| CMP-WGT-004 | Daily aggregation, then linking | Weight compatible daily portfolio returns and link daily composite periods. Its membership observation/timing policy must be explicit. This method is not numerically assumed equivalent to a monthly weighted average of portfolio monthly TWRs. |
| CMP-WGT-005 | Equal-weight and analytical alternatives | Support clearly identified comparative analysis, not substitution for an asset-weighted actual-strategy GIPS return. Maintain separate series identities, labels and publication rules. |

**CMP-RET-005.** The method registry validates allowed combinations of membership frequency, return frequency and weighting method. Changing any of these is a financial-method change, not a presentation preference. A new engine version may reproduce an old method or introduce a new one; classify which before deployment.

**CMP-RET-006.** At each calculated period, sum of eligible weights must equal one within the registered numerical tolerance; sum of unrounded member contributions must equal the composite return. Round only under a versioned policy. Preserve pre-display precision and return a reconciliation residual when independently rounded rows do not add exactly.

**CMP-RET-007.** Detect duplicate portfolio facts, incompatible fee views, mixed native currencies, overlapping periods, non-finite values, zero/negative weighting assets and mismatched universe versions before computation. Short positions inside a positive-NAV portfolio do not automatically invalidate it. Zero/negative NAV and leveraged strategies require a specifically approved method, not absolute-value weights or discarded loss observations.

### 9.3 Linking, horizons and continuity

**CMP-RET-008.** Link actual period results using their historical eligible sets:

```text
R[a,b] = product_(M=a..b)(1 + R_M) - 1
```

An exclusion in April affects April, not the portfolio's valid January–March contribution. YTD is a chain of monthly composite results, not a recalculation using today's eligible portfolio set.

**CMP-RET-009.** Support arbitrary valid date ranges and standard 1M, 3M, 6M, MTD, QTD, YTD, 1Y, 3Y, 5Y, 10Y, calendar-year, fiscal-year and since-inception horizons. Each horizon declares its exact boundaries, calendar, source frequency, completeness and annualization. MTD/intraday output requires appropriate source observations and is provisional until its observation window closes.

**CMP-RET-010.** Return cumulative and annualized fields separately. For `N` complete monthly periods, the approved monthly-basis annualization is `(1 + R)^(12/N) - 1`; for exact-date annualization use a versioned year-fraction convention. Do not mix conventions between numerator, benchmark and risk. GIPS publication suppresses annualized returns for periods shorter than one year; a full calendar-year return is already a one-year return. [G1]

**CMP-RET-011.** Track three different failures: a missing observation in an otherwise continuing track record; an actual no-member performance break; and a method/currency/history incompatibility. Missing data triggers repair; a genuine performance break creates separate segments. Neither permits silent multiplication across the gap. A horizon crossing a blocked period is unavailable as a complete horizon even if later periods can be calculated separately.

**CMP-RET-012.** Return structured reasons such as `HISTORY_TOO_SHORT`, `MISSING_PERIOD`, `TRACK_RECORD_BREAK`, `UNIVERSE_INCOMPLETE`, `FEE_BASIS_MISMATCH`, `SOURCE_CONFLICT`, `INVALID_DENOMINATOR`, `METHOD_NOT_APPROVED` and `METRIC_NOT_APPLICABLE`. Zero is a valid observed return, never a default error representation.

**CMP-RET-013.** Handle exactly -100% without a logarithm crash; treat below -100%, negative growth factors, denominator discontinuities and leveraged economic resets under explicit numerical-domain policies. Do not apply log-based linking, smoothing or growth ratios outside their domain. Preserve valid losses rather than censoring them.

**CMP-RET-014.** A series snapshot pins every monthly result version. A corrected March changes all dependent cumulative horizons and applicable rolling metrics, not unrelated January-only reports. Preserve each old series snapshot for historical reporting.

### 9.4 MWR and alternative strategies

**CMP-MWR-001.** Support pooled composite MWR/IRR from dated external flows and beginning/terminal valuations where the elected method and standards context permit it. Use the performance solver's sign convention, root domain and date basis. Never asset-weight individual IRRs to obtain a composite IRR, and never geometrically link subperiod IRRs as if they were TWRs.

**CMP-MWR-002.** For an investor-perspective cash-flow vector `C_j`, solve `sum_j C_j / (1+x)^tau_j = 0`, retaining residual, convergence, selected root policy and any multiple/no-root condition. Annualization/de-annualization must preserve the actual interval. Apply the GIPS eligibility test for presenting MWR rather than offering a free TWR/MWR switch in an official report. [G1, provision 1.A.35]

**CMP-MWR-003.** For private-capital composites, support vintage, commitments, paid-in capital, distributions, residual NAV, DPI, RVPI and TVPI, plus approved public-market-equivalent methods when appropriate market data and licenses exist. Subscriptions, distributions, recallable amounts, subscription lines and fee/carry treatment require explicit policies and method fixtures. These are additional analytical capabilities, not universal required metrics for liquid discretionary composites.

## 10. Benchmarks, currencies and fees

### 10.1 Benchmark management

**CMP-BMK-001.** Maintain benchmark identity, description, currency, total/price-return type, tax basis, hedging, source, license, calendar, inception, effective assignment and revision history. Support single indexes, blended indexes, dynamic allocations, target/hurdle series and approved custom benchmarks. Official comparison follows the appropriate total-return or documented no-appropriate-benchmark treatment. [G3]

**CMP-BMK-002.** Blends define component weights, effective dates, rebalancing frequency, cash handling and missing-component rules. Weights that are fixed at rebalance dates and then drift are different from weights reset every day. Benchmark construction and all FX normalization must be reproducible from pinned observations.

**CMP-BMK-003.** A benchmark change records the old/new series, effective date, rationale, historical presentation method and approval. Preserve the as-reported comparison. Do not retrospectively choose whichever index makes the composite look better. Support both originally assigned and explicitly labelled research-comparison views. [G3]

**CMP-BMK-004.** Compute arithmetic active return `R_C - R_B` and geometric relative return `(1 + R_C)/(1 + R_B) - 1` as different metrics. Report units and labels; do not call both “excess return” without qualification. A benchmark growth factor of zero makes the geometric ratio unavailable.

### 10.2 Currency handling

**CMP-FX-001.** Keep portfolio reference currency, composite native currency, source-money currency and report currency separate. Convert member weighting assets at the defined beginning valuation fixing; convert external flows at their approved economic-date fixing. Neither a requested currency label nor an FX return alone proves monetary conversion.

**CMP-FX-002.** For a valid native-currency return and FX quote `q_t = reporting currency units per native unit`, an unhedged return translation is `1 + r_report = (1 + r_native) * q_end/q_begin`. Retain quote direction, observations, triangulation, calendar and precision. A hedged strategy requires hedging economics; do not label a simple translation “hedged.”

**CMP-FX-003.** Multi-period currency conversion must reconcile with the approved return and cash-flow timing method. Do not sum AUM across currencies without conversion. A native-currency strategy change requires explicit membership/history treatment; a display conversion must not reset inception.

### 10.3 Gross, actual-net and model-net performance

**CMP-FEE-001.** Expose separate gross-of-investment-management-fee, actual-net, and model-net series; each has a detailed cost taxonomy including transaction costs, management/advisory fees, performance fees, custody/admin fees, indirect fund costs, withholding taxes, rebates and bundled fees. Reject ambiguous “gross” uploads. GIPS gross/net and applicable wrap-fee treatment must be checked by the standards profile. [G1; G5]

**CMP-FEE-002.** Net actual returns use actual fee accounting with an approved accrual/debit and external-flow treatment. A fee deduction cannot be treated as an external withdrawal merely to remove its performance impact. Zero-fee and fee-waiver periods remain explicit.

**CMP-FEE-003.** Model-net calculation supports flat/tiered/AUM-linked schedules, effective changes and approved accrual frequency. Each method must specify whether an annual fee is an effective rate, nominal charge, average-assets charge or another convention. For the explicitly elected periodic wealth-haircut method, `1+r_net,t = (1+r_gross,t)*(1-f_t)`; this is not a universal shortcut for actual fee accounting.

**CMP-FEE-004.** Enforce the adopted standards policy for model fees and prospective-client presentation. Prevent under-deduction intended to overstate net performance. Preserve actual and model fees independently; do not infer missing net returns from a management-fee percentage without the full calculation convention.

**CMP-FEE-005.** Provide fee-drag analytics by period and horizon, with an explicitly additive or geometric definition. Gross/net member sets must be comparable; missing net data is a data issue, not authorization to select only portfolios with convenient net history. Tax-specific analytics carry a jurisdiction/method label and must not be presented as GIPS-mandated after-tax measures.

**CMP-FEE-006.** For ordinary GIPS gross-of-fees and net-of-fees views, transaction costs remain reflected in returns; gross does not mean before every investment cost. The specialized bundled/wrap-fee profile must resolve actual versus permitted estimated transaction costs and required fee deductions using the applicable guidance. A pre-transaction-cost analytical series requires a separate label and cannot be silently promoted to the ordinary gross view. [G1, G5]

## 11. Freezing, overrides, correction and restatement

### 11.1 Required business operations

“Overwrite” is a user action with several distinct meanings. The implementation must expose the following controlled operations rather than one generic update endpoint.

| ID | Operation | Required behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| CMP-CTL-001 | Replace an unpublished draft | Allow revision under optimistic concurrency; retain actor, previous hash, new hash and reason. Invalidate dependent approvals. |
| CMP-CTL-002 | Freeze a result | Freeze an exact composite/period/fee/currency/method/version, a period range or an approved publication bundle. Freezing locks official selection, not source ingestion. |
| CMP-CTL-003 | Freeze imported history | Accept a validated legacy series as external-attested authority with evidence depth; freeze it without inventing a Lotus-calculated result. |
| CMP-CTL-004 | Unfreeze/reopen | Require a reason, authorization, approval, expected version and affected-scope preview. Reopening permits a new candidate; it never edits the frozen original. |
| CMP-CTL-005 | Correct source facts | Send or accept a versioned source-owner correction; recompute only affected facts and dependants. Preserve old source revisions. |
| CMP-CTL-006 | Correct membership | Correct an actual historical classification/data error through a new membership revision. Distinguish it from prohibited retroactive policy redesign. |
| CMP-CTL-007 | Override a rule outcome | Time-bound portfolio/rule/period override with original machine result, permitted reason, evidence and independent approval. Standards guardrails still apply. |
| CMP-CTL-008 | Override a portfolio return | Replace the selected observation with an authoritative correction or evidenced externally attested value; retain calculated and replacement values. Trigger downstream impact. |
| CMP-CTL-009 | Override a composite return | Allow only an evidenced correction/import-authority workflow for official use. Unsupported manual financial values remain non-official and cannot be made GIPS-reportable merely by approval. |
| CMP-CTL-010 | Replace an engine/method version | Run old/new methods in parallel, classify correction versus prospective method change, approve impact and preserve historical replay. |
| CMP-CTL-011 | Restate official results | Create an immutable replacement generation and new series/report snapshots, with materiality, approval, disclosures and distribution actions. |
| CMP-CTL-012 | Withdraw a publication | Mark the exact report/result unavailable for current use with reason and notice; retain the historical artifact and recipients. Do not rewrite a report already delivered. |
| CMP-CTL-013 | Revert a mistaken correction | Issue a new approved generation selecting or reproducing the prior valid economics. Never erase intervening versions or approvals. |
| CMP-CTL-014 | Bulk correction/freeze | Preview all affected objects, enforce row-level authority and define atomic versus per-item semantics. Include partial-failure recovery and reconciliation. |

### 11.2 Lifecycle and precedence

**CMP-CTL-015.** Model calculation, approval, publication and protection as separate state axes. A typical workflow is `draft calculation → validation → independent approval → official publication → frozen`; correction runs beside it as a candidate. Freeze must also be possible on already-approved historical records and imported series.

**CMP-CTL-016.** Define precedence explicitly: an approved current authority selection is not “the record with the latest timestamp.” Preserve default `LATEST_APPROVED`, exact/as-reported views and independent candidate/latest-calculated views. An unfinished replacement must not silently replace the official result or be represented as final.

**CMP-CTL-017.** An override instruction identifies exact target fields/periods, fee basis/currency, source and proposed values, reason, supporting artifact, author, approver, effective/expiry times, materiality, affected consumers and content hash. Expiry creates an exception/reassessment; it must not unexpectedly rewrite historical official performance.

**CMP-CTL-018.** A composite-level return replacement must not leave apparently reconciled constituent contribution or attribution behind. Preserve the original underlying calculation, expose the override difference, and either supply valid replacement decomposition or mark decomposition unavailable for that selected official value. Never pro-rate an unexplained difference over members to manufacture reconciliation.

**CMP-CTL-019.** Distinguish a formatting-only correction from a financial correction. Formatting changes generate new report artifacts but do not invent new financial results. A corrected benchmark or disclosure can be material even when the composite return is unchanged.

**CMP-CTL-020.** Freezing selected historical periods must not prevent calculation of later months. Later cumulative metrics pin the approved frozen historical versions; if a dependency is corrected, produce a candidate series and visible stale/impact status until approved.

### 11.3 Materiality, impact and disclosure

**CMP-ERR-001.** Configure materiality by metric, absolute/basis-point/relative size, cumulative impact, client significance, disclosure omission and business context. Quantitative tolerances are not the sole determinant; qualitative material errors need an escalation route.

**CMP-ERR-002.** A correction case computes a dependency impact graph: source record → portfolio result → membership where relevant → composite periods → cumulative/rolling analytics → reports → recipients. Show old/new values, changes in reasons/counts, earliest impacted date and unaffected scope.

**CMP-ERR-003.** Apply a documented GIPS error-correction workflow for material errors, including required recipients and verifier handling, using a recipient registry rather than a generic email list. [G2, provision 1.A.20] Non-material decisions must still retain investigation and policy evidence; materiality must not suppress source-data truth.

**CMP-ERR-004.** Preserve delivery obligations separately from technical retries. Log each affected recipient class, issued version, notification, acknowledgement if available, failed delivery, escalation and completion decision. A successful download is not evidence that every required recipient received the correction.

**CMP-ERR-005.** If an official result is known to be unreliable, expose an explicit warning and block fresh unqualified distribution pending the approved response. Freeze must not hide a known material error. Emergency withdrawal and controlled reissue must work without destructive database edits.

**CMP-ERR-006.** Do not correct an old monthly return by booking an arbitrary offset into the current month. Source correction belongs to its economic period, followed by governed restatement or the documented non-material publication policy.

## 12. GIPS-aware control framework

### 12.1 Standards authority and claim boundary

**CMP-GIP-001.** Treat official GIPS guidance as the authority, not the inherited source requirements or vendor marketing. Maintain the applicable GIPS Standards for Firms, handbook, errata, final Guidance Statements and authoritative Q&As as a versioned applicability register. Software supports a firm's compliance; a composite or software installation does not independently establish firm-wide compliance. [G0, G1, G2]

**CMP-GIP-002.** Before enabling a standards-bound profile, a designated compliance/methodology owner must approve the register's applicability and evidence. Store provision/topic, authoritative reference, effective date, affected product contexts, implementation control, test, responsible owner, interpretation and review date. “Not applicable” needs a reason, not an unchecked box.

**CMP-GIP-003.** The primary target is GIPS Standards for Firms with composite TWR reporting. MWR, pooled-fund, private-market, wrap-fee and overlay capabilities must activate their applicable controls. Separate asset-owner or other regimes must not reuse a Firms label without their own applicability assessment. All additional analytics remain subject to fair presentation and must not be labelled GIPS-required simply because Lotus calculates them.

**CMP-GIP-004.** Maintain distinct `ACTUAL_STRATEGY`, `INTERNAL_COHORT`, `MODEL_SIMULATION` and `GIPS_REPORT` presentation contexts. No context may misrepresent fabricated or simulated history as actual performance. Research flexibility is not a route around official controls; publication permissions and disclaimers must be enforced at API and report levels.

### 12.2 Mandatory control mapping

This is an implementation map, not a reproduction of the standards. The owner must validate the complete authoritative applicability register, including requirements not enumerated here.

| ID | Control area / authoritative anchor | Lotus implementation obligation |

| CMP-GIP-006 | Composite coverage and discretion — section 3 [G1] | Reconcile the actual managed universe against all applicable composite definitions and permissible exceptions. Keep nondiscretionary, excluded and unassigned populations inspectable. |

| CMP-GIP-011 | History and continuity — sections 1 and 4 [G1] | Validate required presentation length, inception/termination stubs, discontinuities and permissible history linkage. Do not equate a software migration with portable performance. |

**CMP-GIP-019.** GIPS-required annual internal dispersion is based on full-year member portfolio annual returns; it is different from month-by-month cross-sectional dispersion and from composite time-series volatility. Required three-year ex-post risk uses 36 monthly observations, with the applicable exceptions/disclosures. Configure presentation accordingly rather than substituting daily volatility. [G1, section 4]

**CMP-GIP-020.** Build the annual TWR report history from the applicable five-year/since-inception starting requirement toward ten years, and retain longer history when available. Support appropriate small-composite count/dispersion presentation and partial periods without invented data. The report validator must use the exact authoritative conditions, not a generic minimum-count rule. [G1, section 4]

**CMP-GIP-021.** Source exclusions for accreditation, deceased/incapacity flags, fee status, drift, cash, blocks and reference-currency changes are not automatically GIPS rules. Require a documented strategy/discretion basis and a permissible timing policy. A rule that fails this review may be used only in a correctly labelled internal cohort, while actual-strategy composite coverage remains intact. [G2, section 3]

**CMP-GIP-022.** Approving a recalculation does not authorize retroactive composite redefinition or performance-improving policy changes. Distinguish correction of facts under the originally applicable policy, a permissible historical reconstruction, and a prospective strategy change. Preserve the decision and original series. [G2, section 3]

### 12.3 Standards change management

**CMP-GIP-023.** Provide a watchlist and human-reviewed workflow for guidance changes: discover → classify final/draft → assess applicability → impact simulation → methodology approval → tests/configuration update → effective deployment. Freeze the exact rules used for old calculations. Do not silently update historical results when a standards package changes.

**CMP-GIP-024.** As checked on 4 October 2026, the official Work in Process page lists a trade-errors exposure draft with a comment period of 16 September–15 December 2026. It is a draft, not a finalized rule to hard-code. Track it for review while implementing currently applicable controls. [G4]

**CMP-GIP-025.** Build a verifier workspace with read-only, time-bounded access to complete populations, definitions, rule decisions, source evidence, calculations, financial versions, disclosures and distributed reports. Export machine-readable and human-readable evidence without exposing unrelated clients or credentials.

## 13. Composite analytics catalogue and formulas

### 13.1 Common metric contract

**CMP-ANA-001.** Every catalogue row below is a required product capability when its declared inputs and applicability conditions are met. A deployment lacking those inputs must show the feature as unavailable with a specific reason, not quietly omit it or claim support through a blank report column.

**CMP-ANA-002.** Every metric result contains: metric ID/version, composite/series snapshot, fee/currency basis, exact period, frequency/calendar, value/unit, annualization convention, observation count, required minimum, coverage, benchmark/risk-free/model versions, input evidence, quality and publication status. Sample/population estimators and quantile interpolation are part of the method ID.

**CMP-ANA-003.** Metrics use the exact selected historical composite series, including its real membership changes. Do not recompute historical volatility from today's constituent book. Historical realized risk and current holdings-based prospective risk are different products.

**CMP-ANA-004.** The proposed analytics defaults are monthly observations, sample standard deviation and a minimum of 36 monthly observations for the standard three-year risk panel. Other panels may use explicitly certified daily/weekly/monthly methods and their own minima. “Available” does not imply statistically reliable; show short-sample warnings and uncertainty when appropriate.

### 13.2 Required catalogue

### 13.3 Registered statistical formulas

These are proposed default method definitions, not claims of a unique industry formula. Alternative conventions need distinct method IDs and acceptance fixtures.

For `n` aligned periodic returns `r_t`, benchmark returns `b_t`, periodic risk-free returns `f_t`, and annual frequency `K`:

```text
mean(x)          = sum_t x_t / n
sample_sd(x)     = sqrt(sum_t (x_t - mean(x))^2 / (n - 1))
annual_vol       = sample_sd(r) * sqrt(K)
active_t         = r_t - b_t
tracking_error   = sample_sd(active) * sqrt(K)
information_ratio= mean(active) / sample_sd(active) * sqrt(K)
excess_t         = r_t - f_t
sharpe_ratio     = mean(excess) / sample_sd(excess) * sqrt(K)
downside(MAR)    = sqrt(sum_t min(r_t - MAR_t, 0)^2 / n) * sqrt(K)
sortino_ratio    = mean(r - MAR) * K / downside(MAR)
beta             = sample_cov(r - f, b - f) / sample_var(b - f)
alpha_periodic   = mean(r - f) - beta * mean(b - f)
alpha_annual_lin = K * alpha_periodic
```

**CMP-ANA-005.** Use `K=12` for monthly and an explicitly registered business-calendar factor for daily. The annualizing square-root convention is an estimator assumption; serial correlation, illiquidity and valuation smoothing can undermine simple interpretation. Do not confuse arithmetic annualized alpha/mean excess with compounded realized return.

**CMP-ANA-006.** Risk-free data must be converted from quoted yield to compatible periodic total return under the approved day-count and compounding convention. Do not subtract an annual yield directly from a monthly return. Missing risk-free data makes dependent metrics unavailable, not implicitly zero-risk-free.

For a wealth index with `W_0=1`:

```text
W_t      = W_(t-1) * (1 + r_t)
peak_t   = max(W_0, ..., W_t)
drawdown_t = W_t / peak_t - 1
max_drawdown = min_t drawdown_t
```

Report drawdown as a signed loss or positive magnitude with a declared convention. Sampling affects measured peaks/troughs: monthly observations cannot certify daily maximum drawdown.

For annual member dispersion, construct `x_i` from only portfolios satisfying full-year membership for the selected year. Equal-weight sample dispersion is `sample_sd(x_i)`. The proposed asset-weighted population measure is `sqrt(sum_i v_i*(x_i-mu_w)^2)` with `mu_w=sum_i v_i*x_i` and `sum_i v_i=1`. Register the asset-weight basis; an unbiased weighted estimator is a different method, not the same field.

For concentration, `HHI=sum_i w_i^2` and `effective_count=1/HHI` for valid nonnegative weights. Do not apply this interpretation blindly to leveraged/negative weights.

**CMP-ANA-007.** Capture-ratio defaults use the benchmark-positive subset for upside and benchmark-negative subset for downside, geometrically compound each subset, and convert each to a comparable per-observation or annualized rate using a named convention. Exclude benchmark-zero observations from these subsets; insufficient subset, zero denominator or invalid growth factors yields an unavailable result. Provide an arithmetic-capture alternative with a different ID.

**CMP-ANA-008.** For historical VaR/ES, define `loss_t=-r_t`, the requested horizon, confidence and empirical quantile convention. Expected shortfall must specify boundary/tie/fractional-tail treatment. Require a minimum effective tail sample; no automatic square-root scaling of heavy-tailed multi-period risk. Current-book scenarios must not be represented as realized composite history.

### 13.4 Membership and asset diagnostics

**CMP-ANA-009.** Calculate inclusion ratio against the expected strategy population, not only successfully delivered portfolios. Show denominator, in/out-of-scope definition, count ratio and asset ratio. Unknown eligibility must remain visible in the denominator and quality flags.

**CMP-ANA-010.** Reason-level summaries may count one portfolio several times. Provide a separate deduplicated excluded count. For AUM bridges distinguish investment performance from external flows and assets entering/exiting because of membership; never label an eligibility change “investment P&L.”

**CMP-ANA-011.** Explain outliers and strategy drift using source facts. Outlier detection opens an investigation; it must not automatically remove an underperformer or rewrite returns. Leave-one-out analysis and alternative eligibility cohorts are research outputs with explicit changed populations, not replacements for official history.

## 14. Contribution, attribution and carve-outs

### 14.1 Portfolio contribution to composite return

**CMP-CON-001.** For beginning-asset-weighted period returns, expose each included portfolio's unrounded weight `w_i`, return `r_i` and contribution `c_i=w_i*r_i`. Reconcile weights to one and contributions to the composite return using the engine precision policy. Show contributions in percentage points or basis points, not currency P&L. Report presentation-rounding residuals separately; do not adjust a selected portfolio to force a display total.

**CMP-CON-002.** Support multi-period linked contribution through a named, versioned method. A proposed Carino implementation, where each period's contributions sum to that period's return, is:

```text
R_total = product_t(1 + R_t) - 1
k_t = ln(1 + R_t) / R_t           # continuous limit 1 when R_t = 0
K   = ln(1 + R_total) / R_total   # continuous limit 1 when R_total = 0
C_i = sum_t(k_t * c_i,t) / K
sum_i C_i = R_total
```

Absent membership contributes zero for that period only when the portfolio is authoritatively absent or excluded; an unknown constituent is not a zero contribution. Maintain stable identities across entry, exit and source migrations. Logarithmic linking requires positive growth factors; use an approved non-logarithmic method or return a typed refusal where the method is undefined. Near-zero values require stable numerical evaluation, not division by rounded zero.

**CMP-CON-003.** Provide contributor/detractor rankings, contribution distributions, top-N with an explicit remainder, and contribution by manager, geography, strategy subcategory and asset class where supported by actual evidence. Label overlapping classifications to avoid double counting. Annualized contributions need their own approved transformation; annualized composite return does not imply that ordinary contributions can be annualized independently.

**CMP-CON-004.** Composite-level return overrides and imported composite-only series cannot generate genuine constituent contributions. Display contribution as unavailable, or show an explicitly named unreconciled authority adjustment alongside the independently calculated series. Never hide that adjustment in cash, fees or the largest portfolio.

### 14.2 Attribution

**CMP-ATT-001.** Provide holdings/return-based Brinson attribution for appropriate strategies, with allocation, selection and interaction, and configurable arithmetic/geometric active-return conventions. Support approved factor, currency and fixed-income attribution methods through the method registry. Implementation must include a method/data-availability matrix, not one generic “attribution supported” flag.

**CMP-ATT-002.** For a single-period arithmetic Brinson–Fachler method, define portfolio group weights `a_j`, benchmark weights `b_j`, actual group returns `p_j`, benchmark group returns `q_j`, and total benchmark return `B`. A registered three-effect implementation uses:

```text
allocation_j  = (a_j - b_j) * (q_j - B)
selection_j   = b_j * (p_j - q_j)
interaction_j = (a_j - b_j) * (p_j - q_j)
```

The effects reconcile to portfolio minus benchmark return only under the method's complete-coverage and consistent-weight/return assumptions. Define off-benchmark groups, zero weights, shorts, derivatives, cash and missing groups explicitly. Do not derive a supposedly observed group return from contribution divided by weight when the true group return is unavailable.

**CMP-ATT-003.** Composite attribution must use an approved aggregation of consistent member/group evidence, not today's aggregated holdings applied to historical returns. Distinguish attribution of the composite return, asset-weighted aggregation of constituent attribution, and attribution of a model. These may differ and require different method IDs and reconciliation bridges.

**CMP-ATT-004.** Fixed-income attribution shall support duration/curve, spread, carry/income, selection, FX and residual components where required data exist. Scenario repricing is a separate analytical product. Attribute unexplained effects to a visible residual with diagnostics, not a fabricated factor. All optional factor datasets require licensing and source lineage.

**CMP-ATT-005.** Provide multi-period linking with reconciliation to the stated arithmetic or geometric excess return. Do not mix a geometric excess-return headline with arithmetic linked effects without an explicit bridge. Fees, taxes and FX effects must match the return view and benchmark convention.

### 14.3 Asset-class sleeves and actual carve-outs

**CMP-CAR-001.** Distinguish asset-class contribution, asset-class sleeve return, and a separately maintained carve-out composite. An asset-class report does not by itself create a valid strategy track record.

**CMP-CAR-002.** Support effective-dated classifications, trade-date positions, income/receivables, financing, allocation of external flows, internal transfers, cash allocation and fees. A transfer between sleeves is external to a sleeve but internal to the full portfolio. Sleeve transactions and valuations must reconcile to portfolio totals without double counting.

**CMP-CAR-003.** Implement separate actual-cash and allocated-cash carve-out policies, with a compliance-applicability check against the current GIPS provisions and guidance. Retain the allocation method, effective dates, standalone-portfolio relationships, disclosures and reconstruction evidence. An approval cannot turn a hypothetical asset allocation into an actual carve-out. [G1, G2]

**CMP-CAR-004.** Support weighted roll-up from disjoint sleeves using consistent period methodology. Geometrically linked asset-class returns are not additive. Report contributions where the question is “what explains total return,” and sleeve returns where the question is “how did this sleeve perform.”

**CMP-CAR-005.** Treat negative/zero sleeve capital, leveraged exposures and derivative notional with method-specific denominator controls. A percentage return with an economically meaningless denominator must be refused or represented using an approved alternative measure, with a clear label.

## 15. Lineage and reproducibility

### 15.1 Evidence graph

**CMP-LIN-001.** Maintain a queryable dependency graph from each report cell and metric to the series snapshot, monthly results, member facts, membership decisions, policy versions, source observations, imported file/API receipts, valuation/FX/fee evidence and engine build. The graph must cross service boundaries through stable typed references, not require access to another service's private tables.

**CMP-LIN-002.** Distinguish evidence that Lotus observed from evidence a provider asserted. Record source business date and provider revision separately from retrieval timestamp, local batch ID and file upload time. Hashes attest byte/content identity, not economic accuracy or completeness.

**CMP-LIN-003.** A reproducibility manifest must include tenant/firm/composite context, exact input versions and hashes, normalized input schema, membership and source-authority versions, method/policy version, engine build/image digest, decimal/rounding policy, calendar, FX/fee/benchmark context, interval semantics and deterministic ordering. Record random seeds for applicable simulation methods.

**CMP-LIN-004.** Support reproducibility at three honest depths: raw-input replay; replay from accepted member returns and weighting assets; reproduction of an externally attested composite series. A less detailed migration must never be advertised as having transaction-level reconstruction.

**CMP-LIN-005.** Provide forward impact queries: given a changed transaction, status, portfolio return, benchmark, rule or FX fixing, identify potentially affected membership periods, composites, metric windows, reports and recipients. Distinguish affected, recalculated, unchanged and republished outputs. A January correction can affect later YTD/SI, risk and linked-contribution windows without changing every later monthly return.

**CMP-LIN-006.** Provide side-by-side explanation of any two result generations: added/removed members, changed inputs, changed rule outcomes, weighting deltas, currency/fee/method differences, resulting return delta and non-additive interaction where applicable. A “why did this number change?” response must not stop at “recalculated.”

**CMP-LIN-007.** Replays must remain possible after ordinary compute-job expiry. Separate short-lived execution logs from retained financial evidence. Restore tests must verify referential integrity, immutable hashes and report reconstruction, not only database availability.

**CMP-LIN-008.** Retention is configurable by record class, jurisdiction, reporting horizon and legal hold, while preserving all evidence needed to support retained/presented history. Five-year or ten-year display windows are not automatic deletion rules. Purging must produce a reviewed dependency-impact manifest and must not leave apparently reproducible results with broken evidence.

**CMP-LIN-009.** Provide an auditor/verifier export with a signed or checksummed manifest, schema dictionary, policy decisions, sampling support and machine-readable data. Entitlements and source-data licensing remain enforced. Export integrity is not an audit opinion or a claim of independent verification.

**CMP-LIN-010.** An evidence service outage must not cause invented lineage or publication without required proof. Allow idempotent resumption once the exact pinned evidence becomes available; do not replace a requested revision with a newer one silently.

**CMP-API-003.** Queries must expose applied filters, exact date interval, fee/currency, method, authority, availability, source cut and version-selection mode. “Latest” without a defined authority policy is not a valid official-query contract.

**CMP-API-005.** Use typed Problem Details and stable domain reason codes. Error messages must identify recoverable input/policy/action without leaking another tenant's object existence or sensitive raw data. Distinguish not-found, unavailable, conflicting, unauthorized, incomplete and method-unsupported.

**CMP-API-006.** Provide schema evolution and contract compatibility tests. Decimal-string exact-money fields, percentage units, timezone and enum meanings are part of compatibility. A silent change from decimal returns to percent returns is a breaking change even when the JSON type remains numeric.

**CMP-API-007.** API export and UI download must use identical versioned datasets. No spreadsheet-only edits, browser-only approval logic or unsupported direct database maintenance may be the sole way to operate a feature.

### 17.2 Events and recovery

**CMP-EVT-001.** Publish versioned domain events for source acceptance/correction, membership publication, calculation completion/refusal, approval, freeze/reopen, restatement, report generation/delivery and standards-policy updates. Events include scoped IDs, immutable revision/digest, effective dates, correlation/causation and minimal safe payload.

**CMP-EVT-002.** Use transactional outbox or equivalent durable publication. Design for at-least-once transport with idempotent consumers and economically once-only publication. Do not assume queue-level “exactly once” eliminates side-effect duplication.

**CMP-EVT-003.** Ordering is required within the appropriate tenant/composite/authority scope, not globally across all portfolios. Old events cannot replace newer approved authority. Event replay must respect original effective dates, source versions and frozen history.

**CMP-EVT-004.** A dead-letter or retry-exhausted item remains visible and owned, with reason, retry history and a safe recovery command. Recovery requires a new attempt identity where necessary; changing configuration silently under an old immutable job is prohibited.

### 17.3 End-to-end month close

**CMP-OPS-001.** Implement this orchestrated workflow with inspectable checkpoints: establish source cutoff and expected universe; ingest/reconcile; evaluate eligibility; resolve eligible member returns and weights; calculate/reconcile; generate analytics; run standards/publication validations; prepare review pack; obtain approval; publish/freeze a series snapshot; render/distribute; record delivery evidence. Each checkpoint identifies inputs, outputs, blockers and the responsible actor/service.

**CMP-OPS-002.** Allow parallel independent composites and safe partial operational progress. Official release of a requested report family is governed by its atomicity policy: either all required tracks/metrics are ready, or the published output explicitly identifies allowed unavailable sections. It must never silently omit a failed strategy.

**CMP-OPS-003.** Preview impact before bulk changes, cancellation and retry. Cancellation stops uncommitted work without deleting already committed evidence. A cancelled report delivery does not undo a published calculation or erase a recipient's prior receipt.

**CMP-OPS-004.** Provide playbooks for late files, absent portfolios, unavailable Core, disputed returns, bad FX, corrupted imports, expired credentials, blocked approvals, duplicate runs, interrupted migration, failed archive and restatement after delivery.

Explain inherited defaults and overrides next to resolved values.

**CMP-UX-003.** Before policy approval, show affected portfolios/composites, included/excluded/unknown deltas, threshold boundaries, return/asset effects, standards warnings and before/after examples. ## 19. Scalability, resilience and security

### 19.1 Workload and scale architecture

**CMP-SCL-001.** Scale ingestion, rule evaluation, return calculation, risk analytics and rendering independently. Partition by tenant, business-date range and stable portfolio/composite keys. Pre-resolve shared portfolio returns once per exact input/method context and reuse them safely across overlapping composites; do not recalculate the same portfolio for every composite.

**CMP-SCL-002.** Avoid portfolio-by-composite Cartesian scans. Resolve candidate strategy memberships through indexed/effective-dated attributes, evaluate shared observations once, and maintain an impact index for incremental recalculation. Explain query plans and computational complexity for representative workloads.

**CMP-SCL-003.** Use bounded chunks, backpressure, fair scheduling, leases with fencing, idempotent checkpoints and controlled parallelism. One unavailable member or large tenant must not starve others. A worker crash cannot leave a generation falsely complete or publish only successful survivors.

**CMP-SCL-004.** Decouple interactive reads from backfill/close workloads through authoritative read models and resource budgets. Caches include tenant, version, source cut, policy, fee/currency and authority context. Cache invalidation must never redirect a historical pinned query to current results.

**CMP-SCL-005.** Support incremental dependency-based recalculation and full deterministic replay. For example, a corrected monthly return changes its composite month and dependent windows, whereas an unrelated instrument attribute outside the used classification interval must not trigger an unexplained full-history rebuild.

**CMP-SCL-006.** Store large immutable histories in appropriately partitioned relational/columnar/object representations with manifest integrity, while preserving a consistent authority index. The storage technology is an architectural decision; no single database, message broker or cloud provider is mandated by this document.

**CMP-SCL-007.** Validate capacity independently for member-return inputs and raw daily accounting reconstruction. A benchmark using precomputed monthly returns does not prove the same throughput for daily valuation, transaction or risk-factor processing.

### 19.2 Proposed acceptance workload profiles

These are engineering targets to implement and measure, not current Lotus achievements or a vendor-superiority claim. Hardware, network, software versions, dataset distribution, history sparsity, source latency and cold/warm cache conditions must be recorded. A benchmark adjustment requires explicit approval and rationale; it cannot be concealed by reducing the workload.

| Profile | Required test population | Workload boundary |
|---|---|---|
| S — functional enterprise | 10,000 portfolios; 100 composites; 10 years of monthly history; at least 20 active eligibility rules | Full source-to-report integration, including irregular histories and adverse data. |
| M — institutional | 100,000 portfolios; 1,000 composites; 20 years of monthly history; 50 rules; mean two candidate memberships | Incremental close, large imports, corrections, concurrent reads and reporting. |
| L — scale qualification | 1,000,000 portfolios; 10,000 composites; up to 20 years / 240 monthly observations; 50 rules; mean two candidate memberships | Up to 240 million portfolio-month observations before version/replication overhead, evaluated from precomputed return/status evidence. |
| D — daily/raw-data qualification | At least 100,000 portfolios with a separately declared daily valuation/transaction population and instruments/events per portfolio | Portfolio TWR production plus composite integration; explicitly account for much higher raw-data volume. |
| H — heavy-tail/overlap stress | One composite with a very large member set; skewed tenants; high overlap; closed portfolios; sparse legacy coverage; 10% late/revised inputs | Verify memory bounds, fairness, deterministic retries, privacy and failure containment rather than ideal balanced partitions only. |

**CMP-SCL-008.** The product has no hard-coded portfolio/composite/history ceiling at these profiles. Scaling beyond tested envelopes requires additional measured qualification, not a promise of unlimited capacity. Report logical observations separately from bytes, versions and source raw-data volume.

### 19.4 Security and governance

**CMP-SEC-001.** Enforce authenticated service/user identity, role and attribute-based authorization, least privilege, segregation of duties and tenant/firm boundaries at API, worker, repository, cache and object storage. The maker must not approve their own restricted action even through a different UI/API path.

**CMP-SEC-002.** Encrypt transport and storage using institution-approved controls, rotate credentials and keys, avoid persisted bearer tokens, and support audit-safe secret handling. Sensitive business status belongs in controlled evidence, not unredacted telemetry.

**CMP-SEC-003.** Imported files require type/size checks, malicious-content scanning where appropriate, schema validation and safe parsing. Block decompression bombs, path traversal, external spreadsheet links/macros and formulas from executing. Source mappings cannot execute arbitrary SQL or scripting.

**CMP-SEC-004.** Apply tenant-level quotas, rate limits, query-cost limits and safe cancellation. Restrict broad lineage/recipient exports and support verified break-glass workflows with expiry and post-review; break-glass does not disable financial integrity.

**CMP-SEC-005.** Financial/audit retention and privacy obligations must be reconciled through an approved policy. Pseudonymization and separately controlled identity mappings may preserve necessary evidence while reducing personal-data exposure. Do not erase source identity links unilaterally where they remain necessary for required audit evidence.

Customer-specific unavailable data may make a metric unavailable in that deployment; the product still needs the implemented method, contract, validation and tested unavailable-data behaviour.

**CMP-DON-002.** Complete external-only, Core-only and hybrid journeys must run through registered APIs and approved publication/report workflows, including migration, correction and historical replay. At least one governed externally supplied portfolio-return route and one legacy composite-series route must be demonstrated; the existing retained-Core-TWR route alone is insufficient.

**CMP-DON-003.** Deliver the policy/schema/method registries, numerical oracles, provision-applicability matrix, source mapping catalogue, coverage ledger, API examples, report templates, operational runbooks, scale evidence and restore proof. No undocumented direct database step may be essential to normal customer operation.

**CMP-DON-004.** A qualified firm methodology/compliance owner must approve the active GIPS applicability and policy matrix, evidence and disclosure process. Independent verification, when undertaken, remains a separate professional activity. Lotus must not emit “GIPS-certified software” or automatically claim that an institution complies solely because calculation tests passed.

**CMP-DON-005.** Closeout distinguishes implemented capability, test evidence, target-environment acceptance and commercial comparative claims. The companion vendor table is a research snapshot; superiority claims require current, like-for-like demonstration and cannot be inferred from competitors' undocumented features.
