# Supported Claim Contracts

Supported claim registers prevent demo, wiki, README, screenshot, RFP, security, one-pager, and
commercial material from moving ahead of implementation evidence.

The register is intentionally platform-owned because the pattern applies across Lotus apps:

1. classify each claim as implementation-backed, backend-only, degraded, planned, or unsupported,
2. bind every promoted claim to evidence references and proof requirements,
3. require Gateway/browser validation before product-journey screenshots or client-demo wording,
4. separate committed summaries from local-only runtime evidence,
5. keep unsupported certifications, external-client communication, and unproved product claims out
   of client-facing material.

Use `automation/validate_supported_claim_register.py --path <register.json>` to validate a
register before publishing commercial or wiki truth.
