# RFC-0076 Slice 4 Evidence: Cross-App Contract Adoption

- RFC: `RFC-0076-canonical-front-office-demo-data-contract.md`
- Date: `2026-04-11`
- Scope:
  - `lotus-workbench`
  - `lotus-platform`

## What changed

Slice 4 closed the remaining contract-provenance gap between the governed RFC-0076 artifacts and
the front-office runtime evidence generated by the live validator.

### lotus-workbench

The live validator now emits governed contract metadata in
`output/playwright/live-canonical/live-validation-summary.json`:

1. `canonicalContract.contractId`
2. `canonicalContract.contractVersion`
3. `canonicalContract.governedByRfc`
4. `canonicalContract.portfolioId`
5. `canonicalContract.benchmarkCode`
6. `canonicalContract.canonicalAsOfDate`
7. `canonicalContract.sourcePath`

The validator reads `lotus-platform/context/contracts/canonical-front-office-demo-data-contract.json`
when available, and falls back to a deterministic fallback payload when the platform repository is
not present. This keeps the Workbench validator portable without reintroducing ad hoc smoke
assumptions.

The screenshot index also records the governed contract identity so screenshot packs and
machine-readable evidence point to the same contract version.

### lotus-platform

`automation/Invoke-Canonical-FrontOffice-QA.ps1` now propagates the Workbench validator contract
metadata into the platform wrapper evidence:

1. `canonical_contract` is preserved in `output/front-office-qa/latest.json`
2. the Markdown summary now records the canonical contract and governing RFC

This means the orchestrator layer no longer strips away the contract provenance emitted by the
governed Workbench runtime.

## Why this matters

Before this slice, the canonical portfolio and benchmark were governed by RFC-0076 in source
control, but runtime evidence still relied on repo-level convention. After this slice:

1. the validator output proves which contract governed the run,
2. platform wrapper artifacts preserve that proof,
3. screenshot packs and machine-readable summaries remain aligned,
4. Workbench continues to support a deterministic fallback rather than failing opportunistically
   when `lotus-platform` is unavailable.

## Intentional partial surfaces

`performance.evidence` remains intentionally outside the fully-supported contract surface. The live
validator continues to require a truthful degraded posture there until RFC-0079 defines and
implements the gateway evidence and lineage contract.
