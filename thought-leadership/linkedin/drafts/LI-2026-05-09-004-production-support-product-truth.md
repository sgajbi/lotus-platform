Production support teaches product truth.

Before a system goes live, teams often debate features, scope, design, and architecture. After it
goes live, users reveal what really matters.

They ask:

Why is this number different?
Can I trust this before a client meeting?
Who owns this exception?
Why did this report not generate?
What changed since yesterday?
Can operations recover this without engineering?

These questions are not noise. They are product requirements.

In banking platforms, production readiness is not only uptime. It is also explainability,
observability, recovery, auditability, support workflows, and clear ownership.

The best design lessons often come from support desks, operations teams, front-office users, and
rollout teams. They see where the product meets real pressure.

A system that is easy to demo but hard to support is not enterprise-ready.

A system that helps users understand state, diagnose issues, and recover safely is much closer to
what banks actually need.
