# LinkedIn Theme Map

Use these themes to keep content balanced and credible over time.

## Core Themes

### Product Reality

Posts about the gap between screens, workflows, data, operations, and adoption.

Good angles:

1. why a dashboard is not a product,
2. why front-office users need explainability, not just numbers,
3. how support teams reveal product gaps.

### Portfolio Analytics

Posts about performance, risk, attribution, contribution, holdings, exposures, drawdowns, and
benchmark context.

Good angles:

1. explainable methodology beats impressive visuals,
2. calculations need source lineage,
3. portfolio analytics must survive user challenge.

### Mandates And Governance

Posts about discretionary portfolio management, model portfolios, policy packs, constraints,
booking centers, suitability, and operating controls.

Good angles:

1. a mandate is a contract between investment intent and operating control,
2. exception handling is part of the product,
3. governance has to show up in workflow design.

### Advisory Workflows

Posts about proposal generation, recommendation context, suitability, approval, client evidence,
and advisor productivity.

Good angles:

1. good platforms reduce translation between PM, advisor, risk, and client language,
2. approval workflows need evidence, not just status,
3. recommendations must explain the why.

### Reporting And Evidence

Posts about reporting as durable evidence, not just PDF creation.

Good angles:

1. a report is a business artifact,
2. rerender/regenerate/replay are operating realities,
3. auditability matters before design polish.

### Production Readiness

Posts about non-functional quality: supportability, observability, latency, resilience, access
control, audit, workflow recovery, and rollout.

Good angles:

1. production support teaches what architecture diagrams miss,
2. banks buy trust more than features,
3. observability is a product feature for internal teams.

### Leadership And Delivery

Posts about solution shaping, stakeholder alignment, rollout, adoption, cross-location delivery,
and leading without owning every line of code.

Good angles:

1. technical leadership includes making the system understandable,
2. selling internally is part of platform delivery,
3. strong products need domain, design, delivery, and operations together.

## Audience Tags

Use one primary audience per post:

1. `wealth technology leaders`
2. `portfolio analytics leaders`
3. `front office and platform leaders`
4. `engineering and operations leaders`
5. `advisory and product leaders`
6. `banking transformation leaders`

## Avoid

1. generic leadership quotes,
2. vague AI transformation posts,
3. direct Lotus selling,
4. employer/client-specific anecdotes,
5. claims that cannot be defended from experience or implementation reality.
