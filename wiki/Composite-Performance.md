# Composite Performance

Lotus composite performance groups portfolios under approved strategy and membership policies,
calculates using declared financial methods, and retains the versions needed to explain reports.
The complete product target spans source authority, eligibility, analytics, corrections and delivery.

Current evidence posture: owner admissions are PLANNED; all 321 requirements remain NOT_ASSESSED.

## Delivery and evidence boundary

[Platform #923](https://github.com/sgajbi/lotus-platform/issues/923) governs the complete programme.
[#924](https://github.com/sgajbi/lotus-platform/issues/924) delivers its documentation foundation.
Performance correctness and operations/support APIs are the priority, authoritative Excel results
follow, and UI is downstream. All 40 analytical families and 12 report products remain indexed.

Existing source observations and a registered composite product do not certify the full target.
The single implementation ledger begins requirement-level review at NOT_ASSESSED. Its 18 synthetic
oracles are planned specification tests, not executed API evidence. Institution-specific policies,
GIPS applicability, live integration, capacity and recovery require separate owner approval and proof.
