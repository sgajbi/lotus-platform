# Sales FAQ

## Is Lotus a single off-the-shelf product?

No. Lotus is a platformed ecosystem.

The product surface is delivered through `lotus-workbench`, backed by `lotus-gateway` and a set of
domain-authoritative services. `lotus-platform` provides the shared governance, automation,
validation, and runtime support layer that makes that ecosystem supportable.

## What are we really selling?

At the highest level, Lotus sells a governed wealth technology platform for private-banking and
portfolio workflows.

Depending on the client and deployment posture, that can mean:

- product experience and workflow capability
- experience API integration
- domain capability services
- platform automation, validation, and supportability foundations
- implementation and integration services around the platform

## Who is the ideal customer?

The strongest fit is an institution or platform team that needs modern wealth capability without
accepting the rigidity of a monolithic suite.

Typical buyers include:

- private banks
- wealth managers
- digital wealth platforms
- fintechs embedding portfolio and advisory capability

## How should Lotus be positioned against suite vendors?

Position Lotus around:

- modular adoption
- clear ownership boundaries
- governed API and runtime posture
- faster product and integration change
- operational discipline without full-suite lock-in

The point is not that suites are never viable. The point is that Lotus provides a more composable
and controllable operating model.

## Is Lotus only for firms with large internal engineering teams?

No, but it is strongest where clients value configurability, controlled integration, and operational
governance.

The platform layer reduces onboarding and validation friction by standardizing:

- runtime setup
- ingress posture
- repo checks
- cross-service validation
- operational guidance

## Is Lotus SaaS, private cloud, or on-premise?

Lotus should be presented as deployment-flexible.

The current engineering posture supports containerized, governed deployment models that can be
adapted to:

- shared SaaS-style operation
- private cloud
- regulated single-tenant environments
- on-premise or bank-controlled infrastructure where required

## Why does `lotus-platform` matter in a sales conversation?

Because institutions do not buy only features. They also buy supportability, repeatability,
governance, and delivery confidence.

`lotus-platform` is where Lotus proves that:

- standards are executable
- environments are governed
- validation is repeatable
- onboarding is not tribal
- product claims can be backed by evidence

## What is the implementation model for a new client?

The implementation model should be explained as phased and governed:

1. environment and integration posture
2. data and contract alignment
3. targeted capability rollout
4. validation and operational hardening
5. expansion into additional product and domain flows
